Games

Two small 3D games built with three.js. They run in the browser on phones, tablets and computers.

🚀

Neon Drift

Fly a hover ship down a neon track. Dodge the blocks, grab crystals and survive as it speeds up.

Phone: drag to steer, tap to jump · Keyboard: ← → and Space

Orb Rush
